# Lil Wayne's The Carter 6 Review

Lil Wayne's latest installment in the iconic Carter series, "The Carter 6", is a mixed bag, starting strong, but gradually declining in quality

## Promising Initial Tracks

- "Welcome to the Carter" kicks off the album with a dramatic, soulful boom bap beat, featuring Lil Wayne's signature wordplay and a reminiscent vibe of the series' legacy
- "Bells" samples LL Cool J's "Rock the Bells", with Wayne's non-stop flow and aggressive voice moments making it a highlight, despite initial reservations
- "HipHop" is an energetic track with a decent feature from BigXthaPlug, although lyrics about footwear being feminine raise eyebrows

## Mid–Album Tracks Lose Steam

- "The Days" features a surprisingly outdated, FIFA 12-esque pop-rap sound, with uninspired lyricism from Wayne
- "Cotton Candy" is a fun, albeit unexpected, track with solid cocaine-fueled wordplay from Wayne and Two Chains, but feels out of place in the Carter series
- "Flex Up" initially underwhelmed but grew on the reviewer, with a non-stop flow and decent drop, though Wayne's low energy and vocal clarity hold it back

## Late-Album Tracks Plummet in Quality

- "Island Holiday" is a cringeworthy, Weezer-inspired track with terrible vocals, feeling like a misguided Rebirth 2 outtake
- "Loki's Theme starts" shaky but improves with the addition of drums, featuring solid bars and a guitar-driven beat
- "If I Played Guitar" is a dull, unmemorable track with guitar focus, reminiscent of "How to Love" but lacking its impact
- "Peanuts to an Elephant" is a musical disaster, with a baffling, goofy instrumental and Wayne's repetitive, off-putting lyrics

## A Disappointing but Not Legacy-Ruining Entry

- The Carter 6 is the weakest link in the series, earning a 4-5 out of 10 rating, due to its inconsistent quality and poor production choices
- Despite this, Lil Wayne's dedication to rap and his extensive discography ensure his legacy remains intact
- The first five Carter albums remain enjoyable, and fans can revisit those while acknowledging The Carter 6 as a misstep
